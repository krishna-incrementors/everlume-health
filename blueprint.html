WordPress architecture

How the site grows to hundreds of pages without a redesign

Every content type gets its own template, fields and URL pattern. Pages link to each other automatically through shared topics, so a new article instantly shows up on the right symptom, condition and life-stage pages.

Symptom→Condition→Solution→Prevention→Article / Patient guide→Free 15-min consult

URL structure

Clean, shallow, predictable

  • Symptoms/symptoms/hormones-energy/hot-flashes/
  • Conditions/conditions/perimenopause/
  • Life stages/life-stages/your-40s/
  • Solutions/solutions/hormone-therapy/is-hrt-safe/
  • Prevention/prevention/cervical-cancer-screening/
  • Patient guide/patient-guide/is-my-period-normal/
  • Learning center/learning-center/post-name/
  • Providers/about/meet-the-team/name/
  • Locations/locations/layton-ut/

Navigation

Seven menus, no dead ends

Desktop uses grouped mega menus (Life Stages split into Early years, Midlife and Thriving later; Conditions split into Hormonal, Metabolic and heart, Pelvic and bone). Learning Center and Patient Guide share one "Learn" menu to keep the bar short.

Mobile uses an accordion menu plus a sticky bottom bar with "Free 15-min consult" and "Call". Location pages live in the footer and on the Farmington page, not in the main bar.

Breadcrumbs on every inner page, mirrored in BreadcrumbList schema.

Templates

10 reusable templates

  • Homepage (custom, block-based sections)
  • Category landing (symptom, condition and solution hubs)
  • Symptom, Condition, Treatment or service
  • Life stage, Prevention
  • Article and Patient guide (author, reviewer, dates, sources)
  • Provider profile, Location landing page

Each has editable title, URL, H1, hero image, FAQs, related links, provider reference, CTA and full SEO fields.

Content model

Structured, not copy and paste

Custom post types for symptoms, conditions, solutions, life stages, prevention, patient guide, providers and locations. A shared "Topic" taxonomy connects them, so related content blocks fill themselves in.

Fields for medical reviewer, last-reviewed date and sources make E-E-A-T signals impossible to forget. Editors simply fill the form.

Recommended stack

Lean, fast and easy to edit

Built on Kinsta, as planned. Fewer plugins means faster pages and fewer security updates.

Theme

Custom block theme

Lightweight theme built on the native block editor, with locked brand patterns so pages stay on brand.

Editor

Gutenberg + ACF Pro

No heavy page builder. ACF Pro handles reviewer, FAQ, related links and location fields.

SEO

Rank Math Pro

Per-page titles, meta, canonical, robots, Open Graph, schema templates, XML sitemaps, redirects and local SEO.

Speed

Kinsta cache + CDN, Perfmatters

Server-level caching, edge CDN, image optimization, and script control per page.

Forms

HIPAA-ready form provider

Contact forms collect name, email and phone only, with Cloudflare Turnstile for spam. Health details stay out of forms.

Booking

Your scheduler, linked or embedded

Works with Jane, Healthie, IntakeQ or similar. CTA buttons pass UTM data, and GA4 records the click, never the reason for the visit.

Analytics

GA4 + Search Console

Consent mode, no tracking on booking or intake screens, and conversion events for consult clicks, calls and form submits.

Security

Kinsta + Wordfence login rules

2FA, least-privilege roles (Editor, Medical Reviewer, Admin), daily backups, documented updates.

AI search (GEO)

llms.txt + answer-first layout

"The short answer" summaries, FAQ schema, named reviewers and cited sources give AI tools clean facts to quote.

Technical SEO checklist

Built in from day one

See a condition page
  • MedicalClinic, Physician, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage and MedicalWebPage schema
  • One H1 per page with a clean heading hierarchy
  • Editable SEO title, meta description, canonical and robots on every page
  • XML sitemaps per content type, robots.txt, HTTPS and 301 redirects
  • Breadcrumbs and contextual internal links on every template
  • FAQ section with schema on every page
  • Author, medical reviewer, published and updated dates on all health content
  • WebP and AVIF images with lazy loading and set dimensions
  • Core Web Vitals target: LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1 on mobile
  • WCAG 2.2 AA color contrast, keyboard navigation and focus states
  • Pre-launch crawl for broken links, duplicate URLs, stray noindex and canonical errors
  • Location pages with unique local content, never a city-name swap
