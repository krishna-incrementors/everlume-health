Our approach

How we care for patients

Hormone therapy is one option. It isn't the answer to everything, and you deserve someone who will tell you that.Our philosophy of care

We practice evidence-based medicine and explain it in words that make sense. Then we lay out every reasonable option so the decision stays yours.

Read our story

  1. We hear the whole story

    Longer visits give us time to ask about sleep, stress, cycles and family history, not just the symptom you came in with.

  2. We explain the evidence

    You'll know what the research says, what it doesn't, and why we're recommending a test or treatment.

  3. You choose the path

    Hormonal, non-hormonal and lifestyle options are laid out side by side, with honest benefits and risks.

  4. We stay with you

    Follow-ups to see what's working, adjust what isn't, and keep your plan current as your body changes.

FAQ

Before you book

Quick answers about visits, hormone therapy and where we see patients.

Book a free consult
What happens during the free 15-minute virtual consultation?

You meet one of our providers by video, share what is going on in your own words, and leave with a clear next step. It is a conversation, not a diagnosis, and there is no cost or obligation.

Do you offer hormone therapy for perimenopause and menopause?

Yes. Hormone therapy is one of several options we discuss. Whether it fits you depends on your symptoms, health history, risk factors and preferences, and we also offer non-hormonal and lifestyle approaches.

Where is Everlume Health located and which areas do you serve?

Our clinic is in Farmington, Utah. Patients visit us from across Davis County and Northern Utah, including Layton, Kaysville, Bountiful, Centerville and Ogden. Virtual visits are available for many concerns anywhere in Utah.

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

Most patients do not need a referral. If your insurance plan requires one, we will let you know before your first visit.

Is the information on this website medical advice?

No. Our articles are written and reviewed by clinicians to help you understand your options, but they cannot diagnose you. For advice about your own health, book a visit with a provider.
